# Botany 158 — Tenant Acquisition Campaign (Worked Example)

> Built for FI Property Group. Demonstrates the strategic spine — asset + location +
> operational-fit led, FI master brand as the deliverability guarantor, occupancy-through-renewal —
> applied to a real vacant unit, via the property-letting override of the paid-ads skill.
>
> Reconciled to the brand-vs-asset synthesis: we lead with the central-Lancashire
> labour-and-power wedge; FI underwrites the spec rather than headlining it; drive-time
> reach is table-stakes confirmation, not the hook.

---

## The unit, in one line

158,360 sq ft of available-now Grade-A logistics in central Lancashire — with the power
capacity, labour catchment and spec the Golden Triangle and Logistics North can't match on
cost, fitted to how you operate so it works on day one and you never need to move.

## The objective (theirs, not ours)

Let the unit and keep it let. Occupancy is the only hard KPI. Everything below is measured by
progression toward a signed lease and, beyond it, a renewal.

---

## Who we're actually targeting (account-based, not audience-based)

The market for a 158k sq ft shed is finite and nameable. We do not "prospect an audience" —
we build a target list.

**Primary occupier profiles:**
- Third-party logistics (3PL) operators expanding NW capacity or winning new contracts.
- E-commerce / retail fulfilment needing regional distribution within the M6/M61 corridor.
- UK & inbound manufacturers needing power (up to 550kVA) and 12m clear height.
- Occupiers facing lease events (break/expiry) in the next 12–18 months within ~30 miles.

**The decision unit (the black box — cover all of it):**
Property/Estates Director · Supply-Chain/Operations Director · CFO · Head of HR (labour
catchment) · Head of Sustainability (the EPC A on the slide) · plus the **tenant-rep agent who
builds the shortlist**. At this size ~60%+ of converted leads come via tenant-rep channels.
The agent relationship is a channel, not a competitor.

**First action:** confirm and align with FI's disposal-agent mandate on Botany 158 (JLL +
Trevor Dawson + Box 4 + in-house) before any direct demand-gen, so we amplify rather than cut
across them. (Override hard rule.)

## The funnel we report on

Awareness → qualified enquiry → viewing → heads of terms → lease → **renewal**.
We report progression through these stages against occupancy — never impressions or CPL in
isolation.

## Channel plan (mapped to FI's own tier structure)

Botany 158 is a flagship Grade-A disposal: a full **Tier 2** push on a solid Tier 0/1
foundation. The master-brand work sits at Tier 3, funded separately and run in parallel — it
does not ride on this disposal's budget or runway.

**Tier 0 — every property (non-negotiable foundation)**
Live and best-presented on CoStar/LoopNet, Rightmove Commercial and EG, plus the mandated
agents. Consistent spec, CGI, brochure, drive-time map everywhere. Clean up the stale legacy
Cushman/DTRE listings to fix contact routing.

**Tier 1 — LinkedIn, LoopNet, Rightmove (targeted ABM, not broad)**
Job-title targeting (Property/Estates Director, Head of Logistics/Supply Chain, COO) at
companies showing expansion/relocation signals in the NW corridor, plus the tenant-rep desks
at JLL/CBRE/Savills/Knight Frank/B8RE. Sponsored content + direct outreach from a named FI
person. The asset's operational story, not corporate brand-building.

**Tier 2 — events, PR, social, data-led**
- Co-ordinated PR cadence (Place North West, TheBusinessDesk, BE News, Logistics Manager, EG),
  milestone-based, piggybacking the B8RE/Savills NW commentary cycle.
- Agent-co-hosted hard-hat tours, quarterly.
- A single-asset microsite to Tritax/Panattoni standard: HGV isochrones, NOMIS labour data for
  the Chorley/Preston travel-to-work area, gross-pay benchmarks vs the Midlands, port distances,
  masterplan, and operational CGI (racked, loaded, working — not architectural shots only).

**Tier 3 — master-brand programme (separate line, parallel timeline)**
The FI "developer" repositioning. Real and worth doing — but it is advanced by being
*demonstrated through* a fast, well-told disposal, not asserted in a campaign the DMU isn't
reading. Not funded out of Botany 158's runway.

**Paid search (defensive, high-intent, small)**
Own the unit/park name + a tight high-intent generic set ("warehouse to let Lancashire 150,000
sq ft"). Low volume, high intent. A safety net, not the growth engine.

## The message (override copy framework)

**Lead with the hardest verifiable fact in the order the buying committee is scored on,
because the buyer is a black box.** Above the fold, in this order:

1. **Power & grid** — up to 550kVA, EV-charging readiness. Rated "critical" and the
   fastest-rising location filter.
2. **ESG** — BREEAM Very Good, EPC A. 88% of occupiers now rate ESG regulation important (up
   from 69% in 2024).
3. **Labour** — Chorley's 2.6% claimant count vs UK 3.8% / NW 3.9%; a mobile, competitively
   priced catchment the Golden Triangle can't match on cost.
4. **Spec & access (table-stakes confirmation)** — 158,360 sq ft · 12m clear height · 60m yard ·
   11 dock + 3 level access · 136 spaces + EV · M61 J8 in one minute · 56.5% of UK population
   within a 4.5-hour HGV drive. Necessary and defensible — but *not* the hook; comparable
   schemes already market 85–90% reach.

**Reason to believe — FI as the guarantor clause, not the headline.** FI Construction's
in-house build and the ~1m sq ft Botany Bay masterplan appear precisely where an occupier
prices counterparty and delivery risk: *who stands behind this, can they power it, deliver the
fit-out, and still be running the park in year seven?* FI is the endorsement on the asset —
named alongside the occupiers already on the park (Costa, Greggs, Central Co-op) as social
proof. One developer, demonstrably able to build and run the thing. Not a 40-year heritage
narrative, and not a landlord who disappears after handover.

**CTA:** Book a hard-hat tour / download the brochure.

**The retention thread (what makes this strategic, not just an advert):**
The bespoke fit-out isn't only a feature — it's the retention engine. An occupier who builds
their operation around this specific unit faces real switching costs at the break. We sell that
as a benefit ("a space built around how you actually run"), and FI's long-term backing is the
proof the relationship lasts. Occupancy won at renewal, not just at signing.

## What we deliberately are NOT doing (and why that matters)

- No leading with FI's corporate brand journey — a logistics director choosing a shed doesn't
  buy a brand transition; they buy power, labour, location and fit. Brand earns its place as
  proof of deliverability.
- No lookalike/broad-audience prospecting — wrong tool for a finite, high-value market.
- No CPL/CTR vanity reporting — the client distrusts over-engineering; we report on the let.
- No SEO — the client has a partner there. Off the table, as agreed.
